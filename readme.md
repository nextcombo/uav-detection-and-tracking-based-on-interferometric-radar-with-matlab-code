# UAV Detection and Tracking Based on Interferometric Radar [with MATLAB Code]

In modern society, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have rapidly expanded their applications in civilian, commercial, and military sectors, including agriculture, logistics, professional photography, and delivery services. However, the proliferation of UAVs has also raised concerns about their illegal, criminal, or military misuse, such as smuggling contraband and carrying out terrorist attacks. Therefore, the capability to detect and track UAVs is crucial for preventing terrorist attacks and criminal activities. Radar systems are considered the primary solution in this field due to their ability to operate over long distances and can be adjusted to work under extreme weather conditions and at any time. However, the detection and tracking of UAVs are challenging due to their small size, non-standard flight patterns, and resemblance to birds. This paper proposes a new method based on interferometric radar that measures two-dimensional distance and velocity (radial and angle) through Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) estimation of distance and radial velocity from fast and slow time data, deriving angular velocity from radar measurements, and using two-dimensional distance and velocity information to predict the next position of the UAV, thereby achieving detection and tracking of UAVs.
